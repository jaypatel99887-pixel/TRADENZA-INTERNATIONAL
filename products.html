Agricultural Products

Agricultural Products

Raw and processed agricultural commodities including oilseeds, pulses, peanuts, sesame seeds, and organic agricultural harvests.

Typical Export Terms:

Bulk Vessel, 20ft/40ft FCLs • Jute or PP bags • Phytosanitary Certified

Food & Beverages

Food & Beverages

Sourcing assistance for processed food products, canned Alfonso/Totapuri mango pulps, natural fruit purees, dehydrated vegetable flakes, Indian teas, specialty coffees, and private-label packaged foodstuffs.

Typical Export Terms:

Aseptic bags in drums, OTS cans, retail packaging • FSSAI & ISO compliance

Spices

Spices

India is the historic heart of the global spice trade. We connect buyers with vetted processors for whole and ground turmeric finger, cumin seeds, black pepper, green cardamom, coriander seeds, dry red chillies, and curry powders.

Typical Export Terms:

Machine cleaned & sortexed • 25kg/50kg multi-wall bags • Spices Board testing

Fruits & Vegetables

Fruits & Vegetables

Fresh agricultural produce sourcing for international distributors. Includes Nashik red onions, Bhagwa pomegranates, fresh green grapes, G9 cavendish bananas, green chillies, and fresh ginger.

Typical Export Terms:

Reefer 40ft containers • Corrugated cartons with aeration • Pre-cooled dispatch

Rice & Grains

Rice & Grains

Premium Indian Basmati (1121, 1509, 1401, Traditional) in Raw, Steam, and Parboiled (Sella) varieties. Sourcing support also covers Non-Basmati rice (IR 64, PR 11, Sona Masoori), durum wheat, and nutritious millets.

Typical Export Terms:

10kg, 25kg, 50kg Non-woven / BOPP bags • Break-bulk or Containerized

Textiles & Garments

Textiles & Garments

Sourcing capabilities from Tirupur, Surat, and Mumbai textile clusters: 100% cotton yarns, circular knit apparel, woven fabrics, institutional workwear, hotel bed linen, terry towels, denim, and ready-to-wear fashion collections.

Typical Export Terms:

OEM / Private Brand labeling • OEKO-TEX & GOTS certified manufacturing hubs

Home & Lifestyle

Home & Lifestyle Products

Artisanal and commercial home decor products: Handcrafted brass & copperware from Moradabad, wooden artifacts from Saharanpur, natural jute rugs, cushion covers, candle holders, and lifestyle tableware.

Typical Export Terms:

Export drop-test packaging • Barcoded retail packaging ready • FCL / LCL

Tiles & Building

Tiles & Building Materials

Direct connection to Morbi (India's ceramic capital) and Rajasthan stone belts: Glazed Vitrified Tiles (600x1200, 800x1600, slabs), ceramic wall tiles, full body tiles, sanitaryware, granite blocks, and natural sandstone.

Typical Export Terms:

Heavy-duty wooden pallets with shrink wrap • FCL Mundra port dispatch • ISO/CE

Medical & Healthcare

Medical & Healthcare

Connecting institutional buyers with Indian medical consumable manufacturers: Latex and nitrile examination gloves, surgical gauze, sterile cotton rolls, medical disposable gowns, hospital holloware, and mobility equipment.

Typical Export Terms:

Dispenser boxes & master cartons • EN 455 / ASTM standards compliance

Industrial Products

Industrial Products

Engineering procurement facilitation: High-tensile fasteners, stainless steel bolts & nuts, sand castings, ductile iron fittings, industrial valves, flanges, pneumatic fittings, and precision CNC components based on technical drawings.

Typical Export Terms:

Anti-rust coating, euro-pallets • Mill test certificate (MTC) verification

Packaging Products

Packaging Products

Bulk and commercial packaging sourcing: FIBC Jumbo Bags (Type A, B, C, U-panel, circular), PP woven sacks for agro commodities, heavy-duty corrugated cartons, biodegradable paper pouches, and stretch wrap films.

Typical Export Terms:

Baled or palletized FCL shipping • Custom GSM, denier & printing

Consumer Products

Consumer Products

Connecting retail chains and distributors with Indian consumer manufacturers: Ayurvedic and herbal cosmetics, skincare, essential oils, handcrafted soaps, stationery, kitchenware, and daily household FMCG goods.

Typical Export Terms:

Multi-lingual private packaging • Barcoded cartons • Shelf-stable logistics
